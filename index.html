The Show

A one-man journey through glamour and ghosts. Touching themes of betrayal, suicide, aging, irrelevance, and transcendence, this theatrical monologue takes the audience into the soul of a queen facing the final curtain — and what lies beyond it.

The Performer

Meet the artist behind the makeup — a veteran of the stage, a vessel of many characters, now revealing his truest self. This is his most personal story yet, stripped of glitter but not grace.
